    Digital twin model notes

    What the virtual system represents and how its outputs are calculated
    Synthetic educational model
    Production processFive linked stages: depalletize, fill, cap, label, and case pack. Each station has a capacity, condition score, and work-in-process buffer.
    Performance analysisOEE = availability × performance × quality. Output, first-pass yield, cycle time, scrap, WIP, and energy are updated from simulated flow.
    Prediction logicRisk score combines condition loss, injected-fault rules, and buffer pressure. Maintenance timing is estimated from condition trend to a threshold.
    Lifecycle decisionsMaterial scenarios compare indicative cost, carbon, and recovery assumptions. Recommendations balance footprint with line stability and maintenance readiness.
    FactoryTwin coursework prototype · simulated data only · not connected to PLC, MES, ERP, or physical equipment.Assumptions are illustrative and should be replaced with validated plant data for operational use.
